# 1. Japanese secret to a long life

---

Book: "Ikigai: The Japanese secret to a long and happy life" By Hector Garcia & Francesc Miralles

## Introduction

---

This book presents the factors that distinguish people who, on average, live the longest!

## Flow

---

> A state of flow is the best thing you can find in life. It surpasses every other pleasure you can experience. Excitement may last only a moment and come back to bite you later. But you can experience flow as much as you want, every day.
>
> \- Igor

Flow is the inner pleasure that appears when you become fully absorbed in what you are doing. The activity itself does not matter. You can experience it while working, creating, exercising, or even walking, and maintain it for as long as you remain focused. The key is to let yourself perform the activity in your own way and become absorbed in the process, like a craftsperson completely immersed in carving the smallest details.

To enter flow, choose an activity you already understand at least at a basic level so that frustration does not take over. Slow down and give yourself time to settle in. Focus on every movement, decision, and detail, then gradually make the activity more challenging. Experiment without worrying too much about the result. While walking, for example, feel each muscle move, change your rhythm, jump, twist your body, and play. Let yourself work up a sweat.

Before you begin, remove as many distractions as possible. Turn off notifications and put away anything that might break your concentration. Flow may take several minutes to appear, and a single interruption can pull you out of it. Returning often takes another few to a dozen or so minutes. By protecting your attention and learning to enter this state regularly, you can make any activity deeply enjoyable and experience this pleasure every day for as long as you choose to remain focused on the process.

## Community

---

Build lifelong friendships. This is not about people you will spend every moment with or those with whom you will experience the best moments of your life. It is about people you will find time to meet for tea with, no matter what happens, to exchange opinions and pleasantries. People you can talk to honestly.

Such relationships can be built intentionally, but they need time to truly become established. Try it yourself. Find a group of four other people and start meeting regularly, for example once a week, simply to talk over tea. If someone cannot meet in person, they can at least join remotely. If you do not know where to start, you can always find people in our Community. :)

Community also includes the people in your immediate surroundings: neighbors, family, and coworkers. Do not isolate yourself. Offer to help them with things, even if they do not ask. Both they and you will feel much better this way.

Also remember to smile and say thank you often. These are small gestures, but they can mean a great deal.

## Eating

---

Focus entirely on eating. Notice the taste of every bite. Eat slowly and take small bites so that your body has time to update your sense of fullness. Serve your portions on small plates to subconsciously reduce the amount of food you eat. Eat until you feel 80% full. Also avoid excessive salt, as too much of it increases the risk of conditions such as heart attack and stroke.

## Work for the World

---

Find work that makes you feel that you are changing the world for the better, even if the results of your work improve only the lives of people in your local area. Work in a state of flow, creating exceptional results every time. For example, if you are a baker, make every loaf the best one you have ever created so that it brings a smile to the face of the person eating it.

Do not overthink what your work should be. See what people in your area need. Pick up the tools you have and start working. Talk to the people around you as often as possible and create the best work you can. Over time, you will get better and attract more customers. Everything will come naturally as long as you do not give up.

## Nature

---

Live close to nature. Surround yourself with it as often as possible. Take trips into the wilderness and walk as much as you can.

Grow plants at home not for their fruit or flowers, but with the goal of keeping them alive for years. Observe their development and adapt the way you care for them. The fruit will be the culmination of your efforts.

## Exercise

---

Always practice some kind of sport. Experiment until you find what gives you the most enjoyment. Do not limit yourself to a typical gym workout. Go on long walks and create your own disciplines that help you enter a state of flow. Do not restrict yourself to rules that everyone already knows.

## Never Retire

---

Choosing to leave behind what gives your life meaning is equivalent to voluntarily submitting to execution. Never stop doing what you love.
