# 3. Elon Musk’s Five-Step Algorithm

---

Book: "Elon Musk" by Waltera Isaacsona

## Five-Step Algorithm

---

The Order Matters. Start with step 1 and implement each subsequent step only after completing the previous ones.

1. Question every requirement. For each requirement, add the full name of the person who introduced it. This applies whether we are talking about matters within the company or decisions made at the national or international level.

2. Eliminate everything that is not essential, from components such as screws to procedures. Focus on testing to identify as many such elements as possible. If you have not restored at least 10% of the things you removed, it means you did not remove enough.

3. Simplify and Optimize

4. Accelerate the Production Cycle

5. Automate

## Additional Steps

---

- The only rules we accept are those dictated by the laws of physics. Everything else is just a recommendation.

- All managers of technical departments must have hands-on experience.
  - For example, software engineering managers must spend at least 20% of their time actually coding.

- Friendships at work are discouraged. The more we like someone, the less critical we are of them.

- There is nothing wrong with making mistakes, as long as you do not cling to them.

- Do not tell others to do something if you are not willing to do it yourself.

- If you have a problem with something, do not speak only with people higher up in the hierarchy. Also talk to people below you to understand the reasons behind the situation.

- Hire people with the right attitude. Skills can be taught, but attitude is difficult to change.
